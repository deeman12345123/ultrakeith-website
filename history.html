History

Forty-two years of Kool Keith, one era at a time. Each one has its records, personas and videos, plus photos from ultrakeith.net, the fan site that kept track of it all from 2007 to 2015.

1984

Activity and the New York City Breakers

Keith Thornton grew up in the Bronx, and before he rapped he danced. In the early 1980s he was Activity, a popper with the New York City Breakers. The crew danced on TV and performed for President Reagan.

In 1984 he started rapping as Kool Keith. Activity is persona number one on this site.

  • 1963Born Keith Matthew Thornton on 7 October in New York City.
  • Early 80sPops as Activity with the New York City Breakers.
  • 1984Starts rapping as Kool Keith and forms Ultramagnetic MCs.
1984-94

Ultramagnetic MCs

Kool Keith, Ced Gee, Moe Love and TR Love started Ultramagnetic MCs in the Bronx. Their first album, Critical Beatdown, came out in 1988. Public Enemy's producers, the Bomb Squad, have named Ultramagnetic's records as a big influence, and The Prodigy later built "Smack My Bitch Up" on a vocal from "Give the Drummer Some".

Keith's characters started here too: Exotron on Marley Marl's radio show, Poppa Large, Rhythm X and the Fourth Horseman.

  • 1984Ultramagnetic MCs start in the Bronx.
  • 1988Critical Beatdown comes out on Next Plateau (4 October).
  • 1992Funk Your Head Up.
  • 1993The Four Horsemen on Wild Pitch (10 August).
1995-2001

Dr. Octagon, Black Elvis, Dr. Dooom

Keith went solo and turned into characters. Dr. Octagon, a time-travelling gynecologist from Jupiter, arrived in 1996 on Dr. Octagonecologyst with Dan the Automator, DJ Qbert and KutMasta Kurt. Then came Ultra with Tim Dog, Sex Style, and the Cenobites with Godfather Don.

In 1999 Columbia put out Black Elvis/Lost in Space, his highest-charting album. Sony, Columbia's parent company, had bought koolkeith.com when he signed, so Keith's own site went up at koolkeith.net. Three months earlier, as Dr. Dooom, he had killed Dr. Octagon off at the start of First Come, First Served.

  • 1995Big Willie Smith EP with Da Beat Terrorists.
  • 1996Dr. Octagonecologyst on Bulk Recordings and Mo' Wax. DreamWorks reissues it in 1997.
  • 1996Big Time as Ultra, with Tim Dog.
  • 1997Sex Style with KutMasta Kurt, on Funky Ass.
  • 1999Dr. Dooom kills Dr. Octagon on First Come, First Served (4 May).
  • 1999Black Elvis/Lost in Space on Ruffhouse/Columbia (10 August). Number 180 on the Billboard 200, number 10 on Heatseekers.
  • 2000Matthew, the Analog Brothers' Pimp to Eat with Ice-T, and Masters of Illusion with Motion Man and KutMasta Kurt.
  • 2001Spankmaster.
2002-15

The Commi$$ioner years

After the major-label years Keith went independent and sped up. Records came out on small labels, often with Keith producing. The old ultrakeith.net called him the Number One Producer.

The characters kept coming: Reverend Tom of Thee Undatakerz, Mr. Nogatco, Tashan Dorrsett, and the Commi$$ioner, who fronted his own run of albums from 2006. Ultramagnetic got back together for The Best Kept Secret in 2007.

  • 2002KHM's Game with H-Bomb and Marc Live.
  • 2004The Diesel Truckers with KutMasta Kurt. Thee Undatakerz, with Keith as Reverend Tom.
  • 2006The Commi$$ioner, Nogatco Rd. and The Return of Dr. Octagon.
  • 2007Ultramagnetic MCs reunite for The Best Kept Secret.
  • 2008Dr. Dooom 2.
  • 2009Tashan Dorrsett, and Project X's Iconic with Marc Live and Tim Dog.
  • 2012Love and Danger, and the first two Total Orgasm mixtapes.
  • 2013Total Orgasm 3.
  • 2014Demolition Crash.
  • 2015Time? Astonishing! with L'Orange.
2016-now

Today

The old characters came back. Dr. Octagon returned in 2018 on Moosebumps with Dan the Automator and DJ Qbert, and Black Elvis in 2023 on Black Elvis 2. In between and since: MF DOOM on "Super Hero", Del the Funky Homosapien in FNKPMPN, Czarface in Stress Eater, and a steady run of his own records.

The newest is No Alias, an EP with Rad Brown, out in October 2026.

  • 2016Feature Magnetic, with MF DOOM on "Super Hero".
  • 2018Moosebumps: Dr. Octagon is back with Dan the Automator and DJ Qbert.
  • 2022FNKPMPN's Subatomic with Del the Funky Homosapien.
  • 2023Black Elvis 2 on Mello Music Group (16 June).
  • 2024Everybody Eats! as Stress Eater, with Czarface.
  • 2025Karpenters (18 April), and the Punk Rap Tour with MC Homeless in spring.
  • 2026Middi Magazine with Emperor Middi (11 June) and Sonic Psychosis with Mike Redman (28 August). Then No Alias, an EP with Rad Brown (9 October).
