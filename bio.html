Biography

Keith Matthew Thornton, born 7 October 1963 in New York City. Rapper and producer from the Bronx.

Before he rapped, Keith danced. In the early 1980s he was a popper called Activity with the New York City Breakers, a crew that performed for President Reagan.

In 1984 he started Ultramagnetic MCs in the Bronx with Ced Gee, Moe Love and TR Love. Their first album, Critical Beatdown (Next Plateau, 1988), left a mark: Public Enemy's producers, the Bomb Squad, named it as a big influence. Ultramagnetic followed it with Funk Your Head Up (1992) and The Four Horsemen (1993). Years later, The Prodigy built "Smack My Bitch Up" (1997) on a vocal from Ultramagnetic's "Give the Drummer Some".

In 1996 he became Dr. Octagon, a time-travelling gynecologist from Jupiter, on Dr. Octagonecologyst, made with Dan the Automator, DJ Qbert and KutMasta Kurt. It came out on Bulk Recordings and Mo' Wax, and DreamWorks reissued it in 1997. Keith has guessed it sold around 200,000 copies. Rolling Stone put it on its 2022 list of the 200 greatest hip-hop albums.

The same year he made Big Time with Tim Dog as Ultra, then Sex Style (1997) with KutMasta Kurt.

Black Elvis/Lost in Space came out on Ruffhouse/Columbia in August 1999. It reached number 180 on the Billboard 200 and number 10 on the Heatseekers chart, his highest-charting album. Back then koolkeith.net belonged to Sony, Columbia's parent company. Later that year, as Dr. Dooom, he killed off Dr. Octagon at the start of First Come, First Served.

Since then he has kept up a pace few rappers match. Matthew (2000) and Spankmaster (2001). The Analog Brothers with Ice-T. Masters of Illusion with Motion Man and KutMasta Kurt. The Diesel Truckers, Thee Undatakerz, The Commi$$ioner (2006), Tashan Dorrsett (2009), Love and Danger (2012), Demolition Crash (2014), the Total Orgasm mixtapes and plenty more. On a lot of them he's the producer too.

Dr. Octagon came back in 2018 on Moosebumps, with Dan the Automator and DJ Qbert. Black Elvis came back in 2023 on Black Elvis 2 (Mello Music Group).

Lately: Everybody Eats! as Stress Eater with Czarface (2024), Karpenters (2025), The Yoohoo Bros. with Dane Uno (2025), Middi Magazine with Emperor Middi (2026) and Sonic Psychosis with Mike Redman (2026). In spring 2025 he toured the US on the Punk Rap Tour with MC Homeless.
