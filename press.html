Short bio

Kool Keith (Keith Matthew Thornton) is a rapper and producer from the Bronx. He started Ultramagnetic MCs in 1984 and made Critical Beatdown with them in 1988. Since then he has put out 79 albums, EPs, mixtapes and group records, many of them as characters: Dr. Octagon, Black Elvis, Dr. Dooom, Tashan Dorrsett, the Commi$$ioner and more. His newest release is the EP No Alias with Rad Brown (October 2026).
